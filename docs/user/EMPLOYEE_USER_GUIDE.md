# GovAI Nexus – Employee & Verification Officer User Guide
**Workflow Operations for Department Reviewers & Field Inspectors**

---

## 1. Employee Dashboard Overview

Government employees, Village Administrative Officers (VAO), and Revenue Inspectors (RI) access the **Employee Portal** to review, verify, and process incoming citizen applications within statutory SLA timelines.

---

## 2. Reviewing Assigned Applications

1. **Intelligent Workload Queue**:
   - Applications are ranked automatically by SLA urgency and AI-detected complexity score.
   - High-priority items nearing statutory SLA deadlines (e.g., 48 hours remaining) appear highlighted in amber/red.

2. **AI Document & OCR Pre-Verification**:
   - Inspect the automated OCR confidence score on submitted certificates.
   - View extracted applicant details side-by-side with submitted scans.
   - If document forgery or tampering is flagged by the Anomaly Detection Agent, review the flagged discrepancies in detail.

3. **Field Inspection & Verification Report**:
   - Enter on-ground inspection remarks, applicant family verification findings, and asset evaluation notes.
   - Select disposition: **Recommend for Approval**, **Request Clarification from Citizen**, or **Recommend Rejection with Cause**.
   - Forward the case to the Taluk Tahsildar / District Officer for final digital signing.

---

## 3. Managing Citizen Grievances

1. View grievances routed to your desk by the Multi-Agent Dispatcher.
2. Review automated sentiment score and SLA clock.
3. Update resolution actions taken, attach inquiry photos/reports, and close grievance with citizen notification.
