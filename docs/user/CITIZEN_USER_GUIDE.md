# GovAI Nexus – Citizen User Guide
**Official Guide for Citizens of Tamil Nadu (தமிழ் & English)**

---

## 1. Getting Started

GovAI Nexus provides a unified, intelligent single window for all Tamil Nadu government services, welfare schemes, and grievance redressal.

### 1.1 Accessing the Platform
- Visit `https://govai.tn.gov.in` on desktop/mobile or download the **GovAI Nexus** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
- Select your preferred language (தமிழ் or English) from the top-right header menu.

### 1.2 Registration & Sign-In
1. Click **Login / Sign In** on the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your 10-digit mobile number or email address.
3. Verify using the 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP).
4. Complete your basic profile: Name, District (e.g. Coimbatore, Chennai, Madurai), and Taluk.

---

## 2. Using the Citizen AI Assistant (Ask GovAI)

1. Click the **Ask GovAI** floating widget or top banner button.
2. Type or speak your question in English or Tamil:
   - *"What are the eligibility criteria for Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai?"*
   - *"வருமானச் சான்றிதழ் பெற என்னென்ன ஆவணங்கள் தேவை?"*
3. The AI assistant responds instantly with verified criteria, required documents, fee structures, and direct application links with citations.

---

## 3. Applying for Certificates & Government Services

1. Navigate to **Government Services** -> **Certificates & Records**.
2. Select your desired service (e.g., Community Certificate, Income Certificate, Legal Heir Certificate).
3. Fill in the guided multi-step form.
4. Upload required proof documents (Ration Card, Aadhaar copy, Property deed). Our AI OCR will automatically check document legibility and autofill details.
5. Submit the application and save your **Application Reference Number** (e.g., `TN-REV-2026-89412`).

---

## 4. Tracking Applications & Filing Grievances

- **Track Application**: Enter your reference number in the search bar or visit your **Citizen Dashboard** to view real-time progress, assigned officer, and expected SLA delivery date.
- **File a Grievance**: Go to **Grievance Redressal**, select the relevant department, describe your issue, and submit. The grievance is automatically prioritized and routed to the concerned district official.
